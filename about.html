Our Story

The Community Book Nook began as a small pop-up library in a local community centre in 2018.

It was founded by retired teacher Eleanor Vance and started with only a few shelves of donated books.

As more people discovered the library, it became a gathering place for book clubs, children's story hours and community events.

In 2021, the Book Nook moved into a larger permanent space so that it could expand its collection and programmes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a lifelong love of reading and learning by providing free and equitable access to books and educational resources for all community members.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a vibrant and inclusive hub where the community can connect, learn and grow through stories.

Who We Serve

Families

Families with young children can enjoy reading activities and story sessions.

Students

Local students can access books and educational resources.

Readers

Avid readers of all ages can discover new books and meet other readers.
